Connect your AI assistant

Last updated: 18 November 2026

You can connect your own AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to your Uphill AI plan and coach.

What this is

Your assistant can read your plan, log a session, estimate a race time, plan pacing and fuelling, suggest shoes, and ask your coach a question. Your coach answers as in the app.

Who can use it

Every runner with an Uphill AI account. It is free for now.

Claude Code (plugin)

The plugin adds the connection and four skills that keep Claude speaking as your coach. Run these two commands:

  • claude plugin marketplace add kylianvo/uphill-ai-plugin
  • claude plugin install uphill-coach@uphill-ai
  • Then run /mcp, choose uphill, and sign in with your Uphill AI account. Approve the connection on the page that opens.

Claude web, desktop and Cowork (plugin; Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise)

  • Open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace, and enter kylianvo/uphill-ai-plugin.
  • Install Uphill AI Coach.
  • Sign in with your Uphill AI account and approve the connection.

Claude web and desktop (connector only)

Use this if you do not want the plugin.

  • Open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  • Enter the address https://api.uphill-ai.io.vn/mcp.
  • Sign in with your Uphill AI account and approve the connection.

ChatGPT

On the ChatGPT plans that allow custom connectors, add a custom connector with the address https://api.uphill-ai.io.vn/mcp. Then sign in with your Uphill AI account and approve the connection.

Other assistants (Gemini CLI, Codex, Cursor)

Add an MCP server with the address https://api.uphill-ai.io.vn/mcp.

If the assistant asks for a token instead of signing you in, create one in the app under Me, AI assistants, Access tokens, and paste it in. The token is shown only once.

What your assistant receives

Your training plan, what you log, the results of the race tools, and your coach's answers.

It does not receive your watch records, other runners' data or Uphill AI's training library.

Your assistant's provider handles what it receives under its own terms.

Limits

Each tool can be used a set number of times a day. Your assistant will tell you when a limit is reached.

Remove a connection

In the app, open Me, AI assistants, and choose Remove next to the assistant or token. It loses access at once.

Rules of use

Do not use the connection to copy Uphill AI's content, to train a model on it, or to resell it. We may switch off access that does.
